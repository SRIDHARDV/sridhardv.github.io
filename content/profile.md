## About

I'm an embedded systems engineer with around ten years of experience, working where hardware meets software. Professionally that means building embedded devices for IoT across the whole range — microcontroller-class nodes running Zephyr or bare-metal firmware, up through microprocessor-class embedded Linux platforms — along with the board bring-up and platform software that goes with them.

The other half is self-taught and self-directed. Schematic capture and PCB layout are how I build my own projects rather than part of my day job: battery management, building automation, IP camera cloud infrastructure, home lab networking, and putting a mainline-style Linux on hardware that was never meant to run it. Designing the board, writing the firmware, and then living with the result teaches you things that reading datasheets does not.

What I'm aiming at: work that pushes the boundaries of embedded technology, and a steady contribution to the open source projects that the rest of it is built on.
